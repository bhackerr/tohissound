About To His Sound

To His Sound is a Christian creative training center and ministering business dedicated to cultivating healthy, biblically grounded creatives who express the heart of God with authenticity and obedience.

We exist to create a safe space where creatives from every background can encounter the true, authentic presence of God. We believe genuine encounters with Him produce lasting transformation, deepen intimacy, awaken holy hunger, and give creatives the clarity to faithfully articulate and release what Heaven is saying in the earth.

At To His Sound, we believe every creative gift originates with God and should ultimately point back to Him. Our mission extends beyond developing artistic excellence—we are committed to shepherding the soul of the creative. Through biblical discipleship, spiritual formation, leadership development, and intentional training, we cultivate artists whose expression is rooted in intimacy rather than performance, and whose creativity flows from obedience rather than recognition.

Founded in 2019 after a phrase spoken during prayer while commuting to work in Covington, Georgia, To His Sound was born from a simple invitation: to respond to His sound. That personal encounter became the foundation for a ministry devoted to raising creatives who faithfully steward the voice, presence, and heart of God in every sphere of influence.

Today, To His Sound serves individuals, churches, and creative ministries through dance ministry consulting, ministry development, leadership training, workshops, conferences, private coaching, cohorts, gatherings, and speaking engagements. We partner with ministries from inception to healthy, sustainable growth, equipping leaders and teams with both practical tools and spiritual covering.

Our vision is to see a generation of creatives who don’t simply perform for God, but minister from His presence—artists who carry both excellence and integrity, whose lives are as cultivated as their gifts, and whose expressions faithfully reveal the sound of Heaven to the earth.

Because at To His Sound, we believe you shouldn’t just produce—you should be covered.

To His Sound — Where movement becomes obedience.
